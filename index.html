MY PROJECT — ZK-SHIELDED-POOL

ZK-shielded-pool ↗

A Solana privacy pool that lets users deposit SOL and withdraw it anonymously, powered by zero-knowledge proofs. Each deposit can be split into up to three chunks sent to different addresses, so simple amount matching doesn't work even at low volume.

  • zk-shielded-pool-solana — on-chain program: deposits, withdrawals, calls the verifier
  • zk-shielded-pool-circuit — Halo2 circuit and tests; source of the verifier and prover
  • zk-shielded-pool-client — Rust backend that sends transactions and builds proofs
